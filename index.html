Yuchong Zhang张羽翀

Researcher at the Department of Robotics, Perception and Learning (RPL), KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Stockholm

I study how people and intelligent systems can work together well. My research spans human-robot interaction with human-centered design, interactive human-centered AI, VR/AR/MR, affective computing, and embodied interaction. I work with Prof. Danica Kragic at KTH RPL.

I received my Ph.D. from the t2i Lab at Chalmers University of Technology, Sweden supervised by Prof. Morten Fjeld, as a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Early Stage Researcher in the Horizon 2020 ITN TOMOCON. My doctoral work focused on human-centered design and interactive visualization for industrial process tomography using AR/MR.
